Privacy Policy

Elf Adventure: A Christmas Story · Last updated 24 November 2026

This app collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and no user accounts. We do not ask for your name, your email address, your phone number or your location, and we have no way to identify you or your device.

What stays on your device

The app remembers which doors you have opened, your settings, and any pictures you color in. All of it is stored on your own device in the app's private storage. None of it leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Children

This app is made for children and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. It contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no links out to other websites or social networks.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries and makes no network connections at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
